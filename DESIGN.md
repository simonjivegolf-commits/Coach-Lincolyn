# Coach Lincolyn design direction

A complete replacement for the previous dark, condensed sports layout. The site now uses a bright, photo-led academy identity, with the original logo and supplied product truth preserved.

Palette: royal blue #143e91, navy #122c50, white #ffffff, action green #9bdd42, pale blue #edf4fc, body slate #50627a.

Type: locally hosted Outfit for headings; Plus Jakarta Sans for body. Sentence case, no eyebrow headings, open line spacing and clear reading order. Display type stays below 6rem.

Composition: a real coaching photograph and the value of consistency lead the homepage. Programme presentation varies with content. The shop presents original merchandise photos, actual supplied prices, fit questions and prepared WhatsApp enquiries. No fake endorsements, qualifications, results or stock claims.

Interaction: keyboard-accessible mobile navigation, native FAQs, colour and quantity inputs, live item totals and WhatsApp fallback links. Nothing sends automatically. Reduced motion and visible focus rings are built in.

Review against the brief: blue, white and green remain explicit. Removed the previous repeated numbered programme cards, condensed display type, all-caps labels and full-page dark treatment. Photo treatment and attached slogan carry the main expressive moment; the rest prioritises scanning and action.

Surface mode: Persuade, with task-first clarity in enquiry forms. Success means finding the right coaching path or product and understanding the next step.
