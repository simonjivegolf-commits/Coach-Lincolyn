Before your first lesson.

Can I start as a complete beginner?

Yes. Tell Coach Lincolyn that you are new to golf and ask about an introductory session.

Where are lessons held, and how long are they?

Confirm the venue, session duration, fees and schedule directly before booking. These details are not yet published here.

Do I need to buy clubs first?

Ask what equipment is provided and what to bring before purchasing clubs. Confirm footwear and any venue dress requirements.

What should parents ask before booking?

Confirm age suitability, group size, supervision, safeguarding arrangements, parent attendance and progress communication.

How soon will I improve?

Progress varies with experience, goals and practice. Discuss realistic milestones; no score reduction or improvement deadline is guaranteed.
